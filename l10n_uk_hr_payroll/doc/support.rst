UK Payroll Support
===================

UK Payroll was developed by UK based Augur Accountant's (Augur Ltd) who
are an Odoo Accounting Partner and a registered & supervised Accountant
in the UK. Augur Accountants are able to act on your behalf with HMRC as
an authorised agent.

This has a number of benefits including:
 - the ability to see both the payroll data and the HMRC Business Tax account
 - the ability to lodge PAYE filings on your behalf
 - the ability to act for you with other taxes and filings with HMRC &
   Companies House
 - to act as your expert for accounting & fractional CFO requirements

However, Augur Accountant's is subject to UK Supervision for Anti-Money
Laundering and as such must risk assess and vette any potential client
before accepting an engagement.

part of the Augur group of companies.
